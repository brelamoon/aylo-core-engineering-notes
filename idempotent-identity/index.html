AYLO Core · Identity architecture

Identity issuance must survive retries.

Commerce and identity become tightly coupled when a purchase should later appear inside a user account. The fragile implementation assumes that every callback succeeds exactly once.

Connect through a verified address

AYLO Core’s current direction allows avatar checkout to begin with the buyer’s email. Server-side identity allocation and the purchase reservation are associated with a protected representation of that email. When the person later verifies the same address inside AYLO, the system can claim the completed entitlement without exposing an internal identifier.

Idempotency is the central rule

Payment callbacks retry. Email confirmations retry. Users close and reopen applications. Every claim operation needs to produce the same final ownership state when it receives the same valid event again.

Reservation and hosted-checkout handoff are the verified boundaries. Successful paid fulfillment remains a separate end-to-end proof requirement.
