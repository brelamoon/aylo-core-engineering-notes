AYLO Core · Public engineering brief

A persistent identity needs more than a profile picture.

AYLO Core is building digital identity through original avatars, streetwear, and connected physical-digital products. The current public layer is the avatar catalog and official checkout handoff; wider identity progression remains product direction.

Catalog boundaries

A file in source control is not automatically a public product. AYLO Core separates review-stage assets from the released catalog and checkout resolver. A release gate should verify asset existence, identifier uniqueness, price intent, image format, and the public destination.

Responsive 3D

The product viewer keeps a static catalog image visible while a GLTF scene loads. Camera fitting responds to model bounds and card aspect ratio. Pixel ratio is capped, reduced-motion preferences are respected, and WebGL resources are disposed when the viewer leaves the page.

What is available and what is direction

  • Available now: the public avatar catalog, current availability, and official checkout handoff.
  • Product direction: broader identity progression, access, participation, and deeper physical-digital links.
